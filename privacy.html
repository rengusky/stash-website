STASH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

The short version: everything you save in Stash stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud. We run no servers, require no account, and use no tracking or analytics. We can't see your data — and we like it that way.

What Stash stores

Stash stores the content you choose to save — links, images, and notes — along with things you add to organise them: folders, tags, captions, and pins. There is no sign-up, no account, and no profile.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Your saved items are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  • In your private iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud, Stash syncs your items between your devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your personal, private iCloud database. This data is tied to your Apple Account, counts toward your iCloud storage, and is encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest. The developer has no access to it.

Stash operates no servers of its own. Your data is never sent to us — we could not read it if we wanted to.

On-device analysis

To make your library searchable, Stash analyses saved images entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks: recognising text in images (OCR), suggesting tags, and extracting prominent colors. None of this analysis leaves your device, and no third-party AI service is involved.

Network requests for link previews

When you save a link, Stash fetches that page's title, description, and preview image directly from the website — the same request your browser would make if you opened the page. Those websites see a standard web request from your device. Stash adds no identifiers to these requests.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of your data to anyone.
  • No third-party SDKs that phone home.

Deleting your data

Delete any item inside the app and it's gone from your library. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. To remove synced data as well, delete the Stash data from iCloud in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Stash is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them — indeed, it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy materially changes, the app will show you the updated summary on next launch, and this page will be updated with a new date.
