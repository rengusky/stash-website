Bookmarks you never open. Screenshots you never find. Tabs you keep “for later.” Saving was never the problem — coming back is.

How Stash works

  1. 1

    Save from anywhere

    Share to Stash from any app in two taps. On Mac, just drag things in. Titles and previews are fetched for you.

  2. 2

    It organises itself

    Links from Instagram, YouTube, Reddit and X file themselves into the right folder. Your own folders get an emoji and a colour.

  3. 3

    Stash reminds you

    A daily nudge at a time you choose: “Time to catch up 📚.” What you saved finally gets seen.

A calm home for everything you save

  • 🔗

    Links with rich previews

  • 🖼️

    Images too

    Screenshots, photos, visual references — stash them next to your links.

  • 📁

    Folders that feel yours

    Every folder gets an emoji and a colour. Design, Travel, Recipes — your call.

  • 🔍

    Search everything

    Find anything by title, domain, folder or keyword. It’s all one search away.

  • 🔔

    Reminders that work

    Pick a time. Stash resurfaces your saves daily so nothing rots in a list.

  • 🔒

    No accounts. Your stash syncs through your own iCloud, across iPhone, iPad and Mac.
