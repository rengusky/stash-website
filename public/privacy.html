STASH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The short version: everything you save in Stash stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud. There is no account, no advertising, and no cross-app tracking. The only thing that can ever leave your device is an optional, anonymous count of what you create — and only if you switch it on. We can't see your saved content, and we like it that way.

What Stash stores

Stash stores the content you choose to save — links, images, and notes — along with things you add to organise them: folders, tags, captions, and pins. There is no sign-up, no account, and no profile.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Your saved items are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  • In your private iCloud. If you're signed in to iCloud, Stash syncs your items between your devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your personal, private iCloud database. This data is tied to your Apple Account, counts toward your iCloud storage, and is encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest. The developer has no access to it.

Your saved content — links, images, videos, and notes — is never sent to us and never leaves your device and your private iCloud. The only exception is the optional, anonymous usage analytics described below, which sends counts only, never your content.

On-device analysis

To make your library searchable, Stash analyses saved images entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks: recognising text in images (OCR), suggesting tags, and extracting prominent colors. None of this analysis leaves your device, and no third-party AI service is involved.

Network requests for link previews

When you save a link, Stash fetches that page's title, description, and preview image directly from the website — the same request your browser would make if you opened the page. Those websites see a standard web request from your device. Stash adds no identifiers to these requests.

Anonymous usage analytics (optional, off by default)

Stash includes an optional setting — Share Anonymous Usage, in Settings — that is off by default. It does nothing unless you turn it on. When it is on, Stash sends an anonymous count each time you create something, so we can understand how the app is used at an aggregate level.

Each count includes only:

  • the type of item created (moodboard, image, video, link, or note);
  • whether it was created in the app or via the Share Extension;
  • the platform (iOS or macOS) and app build number;
  • a random identifier generated on your device, not tied to your Apple Account, iCloud, name, email, or any personal identity.

It never includes any of your saved content — no titles, URLs, text, tags, captions, images, or videos. The counts go to a small first-party endpoint the developer operates (on Cloudflare); no third-party analytics or advertising service is involved. You can turn the setting off at any time to stop sending.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring of your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete any item inside the app and it's gone from your library. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. To remove synced data as well, delete the Stash data from iCloud in Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Stash is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them — indeed, it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy materially changes, the app will show you the updated summary on next launch, and this page will be updated with a new date.
